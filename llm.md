#### Conferences

The "Big Three" Academic Research SummitsThese A*-ranked venues dictate the pace of AI breakthrough discoveries, mathematical theory, and foundational architecture:

* NeurIPS (Neural Information Processing Systems): The gold standard for neural networks and machine learning theory. It boasts the highest h-index impact factor in the ML landscape.
* ICML (International Conference on Machine Learning): Heavily focused on rigorous empirical results, training stability, optimization, and algorithmic efficiency.
* CVPR / ICCV (Computer Vision & Pattern Recognition): The dominant global fixtures for visual AI, multimodal architectures, and video processing.
